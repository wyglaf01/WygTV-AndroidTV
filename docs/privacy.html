Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026. This policy covers the WygTV and WygOTA apps on Android, Android TV, Fire TV, Windows, Mac, Ubuntu, iPhone, iPad and Samsung Smart TVs, including the versions in the Samsung Galaxy Store and Samsung's TV app store.

The short version

WygTV is a player. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not run a server that receives your data and never sees your playlists, logins or viewing history.

What stays on your device

  • Playlists you add: server addresses, usernames and passwords for Xtream Codes and M3U playlists, and the addresses of HDHomeRun, AirTV and Tablo tuners.
  • Favorites, categories, My List, hidden items, watch progress and settings.
  • Your own TMDb or OMDb API keys, if you enter them.
  • A crash report from the last crash, shown to you the next time the app opens. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Backup files, which are written only when you choose Back up and are saved where you pick. On a Samsung TV they are kept inside the app (the newest five).
  • TV guides, kept for up to 12 hours so they don't have to be downloaded every time the app opens.
  • On a Samsung TV, Settings → About → Diagnostics lists the TV model and software versions and the app's recent errors, with playlist logins hidden. It is kept in memory only, shown only on your screen, and never sent anywhere.

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app (except backup files you saved yourself).

Who the app connects to

The app connects directly from your device to these services, and only to do what you asked:

  • Your playlist provider: the server in the playlists you add, with the login you entered, to load channels, movies, series and guides.
  • Your tuners: HDHomeRun, AirTV and Tablo devices on your home network. To find them and load their TV guide it may also contact SiliconDust (api.hdhomerun.com) and Tablo (api.tablotv.com), which receive the tuner's own ID.
  • TV guide links you add, and free guides from epg.pw if you choose one.
  • Movie and series information from TMDb (themoviedb.org), OMDb (omdbapi.com), TVmaze and Apple's iTunes charts. The app sends titles and IDs to look up, never your login or playlist.
  • Subtitles from OpenSubtitles, SubDL and Podnapisi, when you search for them.
  • YouTube, when you play a trailer.
  • GitHub, to check for updates, in versions installed from GitHub. The Galaxy Store and Samsung TV versions don't do this; the store updates them.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and may see your IP address, as any website does.

Content

WygTV comes with no channels or videos. You add your own playlists and tuners, and you are responsible for having the right to watch what they provide.

Children

WygTV is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
