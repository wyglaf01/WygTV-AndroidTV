Privacy Policy

This policy covers WygTV, WygTV Beta, WygOTA, WygDesktop, WygTV-Mac, WygTV-iOS and WygTV for Samsung TVs (together, "the apps"), made by Wyglaf. Last updated November 25, 2026.

The short version

The apps don't have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. They don't collect, sell or share personal information. Everything you set up stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your playlists (including Xtream Codes server addresses, usernames and passwords), antenna tuner addresses, favorites, watch history and progress, My List, hidden channels and settings are stored only on your device. They leave it only when you make a backup file yourself, which is saved where you choose.

Who the apps connect to

The apps don't provide any TV channels, movies or series. They play what you add, so they connect to:

  • The services and links you add: your IPTV provider (Xtream Codes or M3U) and any TV guide (XMLTV) links, to load channels, guides and streams. Your login is sent only to the provider it belongs to.
  • Your antenna tuners on your home network (HDHomeRun, Tablo). To find them, the apps may ask SiliconDust (api.hdhomerun.com) or Tablo (api.tablotv.com) which tuners share your network; the HDHomeRun TV guide comes from SiliconDust.
  • Movie and series information, when you open a title: OMDb, TMDb and TVmaze receive the title's name or ID to return ratings, posters and descriptions.
  • Subtitles, only when you search for them: OpenSubtitles or SubDL receive the title and language.
  • Updates: copies installed outside an app store check GitHub (github.com) for a newer version. Copies installed from an app store don't; the store updates them.

These services see your device's internet address, as any website does, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The apps send them nothing that identifies you.

Permissions

Internet and network access, to play streams and find tuners. Storage, only to save and restore backup files and to open subtitle files you pick.

Children

The apps aren't directed at children and collect no information from anyone.
