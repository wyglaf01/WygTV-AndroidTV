What's new in WygTV for Android

Released versions, newest first. Each one was tested as a beta before it was released.

WygTV 1.0.297

Released November 23, 2026

  • Live TV: pressing OK or tapping a playlist (such as Trex) opens its categories right under it; press again to close them.
  • Live TV, Movies and Series menus shrink to a strip of icons when you move into the channels or the guide, giving the guide more room.
  • Tablets: tap a channel name to preview it straight away; tap a show to open the channel full screen. Long-press for channel options.
  • Channel options: "Restart" is now called Resume, and the Hidden switch is gone (hide channels in Settings › Hide and show channels).
  • Movies and Series search: the search bar sits in the title row, so the posters get the full height.
  • Playlists and TV guides update automatically when added and on a schedule (every start-up, 6, 12 or 24 hours), with the last update time shown in Settings › Manage playlists.
  • AirTV antenna TV: a new entry that opens the Sling TV app, where AirTV channels play.
  • Menus: two-line rows show a bigger title and a smaller description.
