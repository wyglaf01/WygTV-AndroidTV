How WygTV started

Made by Wyglaf
The person behind WygTV

WygTV began with a simple goal: to bring free over-the-air channels from an HDHomeRun network tuner to the TV, complete with a proper channel list and TV guide. From there it grew into a full IPTV player, so your Xtream Codes and M3U playlists sit right alongside your antenna channels in one place. It was first built for Android TVs and tablets, and it has since come to Windows, Mac, Ubuntu, iPhone and iPad as well.

What WygTV can do

Everything below is in the app today. Details on every screen and setting are in the help guide.

☰

Every kind of playlist

  • Xtream Codes logins (Live TV, Movies, Series and the provider's guide)
  • M3U / M3U8 links, grouped the way the playlist groups them
  • Paste a full get.php link and WygTV fills in the login
  • Keep as many playlists as you like and switch any time
  • Playlists and TV guides update by themselves: at start-up or every 6, 12 or 24 hours, with the last update time shown
📡

Free antenna TV

  • HDHomeRun tuners found on your network automatically
  • Each tuner has its own 📡 entry on the side menu, on Home and in the TV Guide
  • Free program guide from the tuner, or your own XMLTV link
▣

Live TV with a live preview

  • Menu on the left: Favorites, Search, your channel groups, then your playlists; OK on a playlist opens its categories right under it
  • Preview window and a 90-minute guide on the right follow what you highlight
  • The menu shrinks to icons while you browse the guide, and ◀ or Back takes you right back to where you were
  • OK plays full screen, hold OK for options
  • On a tablet: tap a channel name to preview it, tap a show to watch it
▦

Full TV guide

  • Classic time grid with channel logos and a live preview
  • Show all channels, Favorites, a category, your groups or Antenna TV
  • Extra XMLTV guides fill in channels your provider leaves blank, including free guides for 13 countries
▶

Movies & Series

  • Poster wall with categories down the left
  • Details page with plot, cast, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic ratings
  • Series remember where you stopped, episode by episode
  • My List, and a Search bar at the top that covers the whole library
★

Make it yours

  • Favorites from every playlist in one place
  • Your own channel groups (e.g. "Sports") mixing channels from any playlist
  • Hide categories and channels you never watch
  • Recently watched, and start-up on your last channel
🎮

Made for the remote

  • CH+ / CH− and Up/Down change channel
  • Number keys jump straight to a channel
  • Info banner with what's on now and next, Last channel, Guide key
  • Menus wrap around top to bottom
⚙

A steady player

  • Reconnects by itself when a stream drops
  • Buffer and video decoder settings for shaky streams or boxes
  • MPEG-TS or HLS for live channels
  • Subtitles and closed captions
◐

Themes, backup & updates

  • Royal blue & bronze, Steampunk, Teal, Emerald, Mahogany, Black & gold, plus a see-through Glass effect
  • "Live TV only" mode for a simple cable-box feel
  • Back up and restore everything to a file
  • Checks for new versions and installs them over itself; big lists load in pieces so it stays steady on small TV boxes
  • Want new changes first? Try the WygTV Beta

🔥 Install on Fire TV with Downloader

Fire TV can't install apps from the web on its own, so use the free Downloader app by AFTVnews.

  1. Install Downloader. On the Fire TV home screen choose Find → Search, type Downloader, and install the orange app.
  2. Allow it to install apps. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps and turn Downloader on.
    No Developer options? Open Settings → My Fire TV → About and press OK on your device name 7 times.
  3. Enter the address. Open Downloader and in the URL box type the code , or https://github.com/wyglaf01/WygTV-AndroidTV/releases/latest/download/wygtv.apk, then press Go.
  4. Install. When the download finishes, choose Install, then Done. Downloader offers to delete the file afterwards: say Delete to save space.
  5. Open WygTV from Your Apps & Games (or hold Home → Apps) and follow the first-time setup.

The same steps work on other Android TV boxes: install Downloader from the Play Store and allow it under Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources. Updates after this come from inside WygTV, so you only do this once.
